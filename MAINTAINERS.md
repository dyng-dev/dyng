# Maintainers

The people who review and merge changes, by area. How to become a maintainer, and how
decisions are made, is described in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Review ownership is
enforced by [.github/CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS).

| Name | GitHub | Role | Areas |
|---|---|---|---|
| S M Shovan | [@SMShovan](https://github.com/SMShovan) | lead maintainer | everything: core, framework, containers, I/O, all algorithms, build, CI, docs, releases |

The Community Moderators of the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) are the maintainers
listed here.

**Algorithm maintainers** (optional; PLAN Section 10.1): the co-authors of an algorithm's
papers (see [AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md)) may be added here, and as reviewers of that algorithm's
folders in CODEOWNERS, when they accept.
