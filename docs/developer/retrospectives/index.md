# Milestone retrospectives

Each milestone ends with a short retrospective: what was done, the deviations from the plan
(each a pragmatic choice with the same intent, and why), notes for the next milestone, and the
open items for the lead maintainer (PLAN Section 0.3). Scope changes and re-estimates are made
here, not in the middle of a port. The M1a retrospective holds the first **re-estimate** of the
roadmap, summarized in {doc}`../plan`.
