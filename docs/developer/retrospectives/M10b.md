### Deviations from the plan and the step's text (each with the same intent)

1. **The operators stay in the algorithm's folder** (`algorithms/label_propagation/operators/`,
   namespace `lp_ops`), not in `cpp/src/operators/` as PLAN 11.4's M10 row lists them: PLAN
   4.5.3's rule of two (one user each); ADR 0102 records the promotion rule.
2. **The operators take the resources and name their executor** (`neighbor_reduce<cuda_exec>(res,
   ...)`), not an executor object: an operator with its own kernels needs the stream, which
   `operators::cuda_exec` keeps private, and M10 does not change `cpp/src/operators/` (the shared
   headers; `execution.hpp` is M11's carried item).
3. **No operators twin of `schedule::in_place`** (PLAN 4.5.4 and the step's text: optional): an
   update with `engine::operators` under `in_place` raises `not_supported_error`. So the in_place
   mutant (`inplace_plain_jacobi`) has nothing to act on in the operators engine; the five other
   mutants fail its own cases (the CTests `label_propagation.mutation.cuda_operators.*`), and the
   in_place mutant keeps its fused and host rows.
4. **compute() is IrLP on every engine** (the published baseline, PLAN 6.4.7: "IrLP stays
   deliberately unchanged"); `engine::operators` no longer raises in compute().
5. **One kernel per phase, and the arithmetic of the fused engine**: the large bin's block is
   1024 threads while the frontier holds at most 1024 vertices (the fused engine's persistent
   kernel takes those iterations) and 256 above, so the labels are the fused engine's bits; the
   persistent kernel itself has no operators twin.
6. **The calibration builds the compatibility tools' algorithms only** (DYNG_ALGORITHMS =
   label_propagation, sssp, cycle_count, mosp, triad_count; a reconfigure per variant recompiles
   label_propagation's CUDA sources alone); the control variant proves the build reproduces the
   goldens' bits.
7. **The stage trace** records the fused engine only (the operators engine's parity is its bits
   and counts).
