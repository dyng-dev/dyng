### The STOP

Criterion 7 asks that on C (5k5 and 50k at delta 1e-6, both rules) every batch has 2000 delta /
mean |F - F*| >= 2 with 0 PCG skips. O's own damped run at 5k5 has 1.32 on batch 1 (mean
1.51e-3 against 2e-3; 869 vertices in ground-truth-free components are excluded there; 0
skips; O's `--check` exits 0). This is the step's go/no-go, so step 2 does not start; the margin,
the bound and the corpus are unchanged (STOP rule: never relax a bar). For the author: whether
the AD7 margin of 2 applies to every batch of C including the first unlabeled batch at 5k5, or
how C should be read; the data are in `parity/results/M10a-check-margins.json` and the logs.

**Decided by the author on 2026-10-29 (GOVERNANCE.md's approvals log, the row "The AD7 margin
on C").** AD7's bound 2000 delta stays on every batch, schedule, engine and backend; the
go/no-go margin of 2 is waived for this one batch only (5k5 damped, delta 1e-6, batch 1),
because the damped schedule is deterministic, so run-to-run noise cannot erode its headroom;
the certificate reports every batch's margin, and every other batch of C keeps the margin of 2.
The STOP is settled.

   `nsys:peak_live` row of label_propagation's map (the earlier maps still refuse it), so the
   map can carry AD2's third gated row.

   bench binaries started inside the window) in their samples: those readings are not counted;
   every case has a counted reading (the first window saw no foreign process; its record keeps
   the first and last sample of each reading only), and all readings give the same peak. The
   other track's jobs inside an open GPU1-QUIET window are reported (open items).

7. **The hub's in-place call** is not reproducible: its outputs (5 runs) are a distribution under

8. **The GCC 13 compile of the recorder** uses the GCC 13.4 compiler of the conda package cache
   with its libstdc++ headers over the system glibc headers (compile only; no linkable GCC 13 is
