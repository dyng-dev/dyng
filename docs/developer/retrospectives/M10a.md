### The STOP

Criterion 7 asks that on C (5k5 and 50k at delta 1e-6, both rules) every batch has 2000 delta /
mean |F - F*| >= 2 with 0 PCG skips. O's own damped run at 5k5 has 1.32 on batch 1 (mean
1.51e-3 against 2e-3; 869 vertices in ground-truth-free components are excluded there; 0
skips; O's `--check` exits 0). This is the step's go/no-go, so step 2 does not start; the margin,
the bound and the corpus are unchanged (STOP rule: never relax a bar). For the author: whether
the AD7 margin of 2 applies to every batch of C including the first unlabeled batch at 5k5, or
how C should be read; the data are in `parity/results/M10a-check-margins.json` and the logs.

**Decided by the author on 2026-10-29 (GOVERNANCE.md's approvals log, the row "The AD7 margin
on C").** AD7's bound 2000 delta stays on every batch, schedule, engine and backend; the
go/no-go margin of 2 is waived for this one batch only (5k5 damped, delta 1e-6, batch 1),
because the damped schedule is deterministic, so run-to-run noise cannot erode its headroom;
the certificate reports every batch's margin, and every other batch of C keeps the margin of 2.
The STOP is settled.

### Deviations from the plan and the step's text (each with the same intent)

1. **`references.toml` `smoke_test`** runs the two unit tests (as the step asks); O's full
   `make test` (73 s, not an hour as expected) ran once and is recorded.
2. **`nvcc_default`** (a new optional key of `references.toml`): the build names no `NVCC=` (the
   Makefile's default), so the build fingerprint records the nvcc that default resolves to; the
   other references have no such key and keep their fingerprints.
3. **ADR 0066** carries the build-flags go/no-go apart from the protocol (ADR 0064) and the
   recorder (ADR 0065); four ADRs (0063-0066) instead of the three the step lists, the numbers
   inside M10's block.
4. **The memory gate kind:** `load_regions` accepts `gate = "memory"` only on the
   `nsys:peak_live` row of label_propagation's map (the earlier maps still refuse it), so the
   map can carry AD2's third gated row.
5. **The nsys windows after the first** saw the other track's GPU-1 jobs (MOSP_ESCHER test and
   bench binaries started inside the window) in their samples: those readings are not counted;
   every case has a counted reading (the first window saw no foreign process; its record keeps
   the first and last sample of each reading only), and all readings give the same peak. The
   other track's jobs inside an open GPU1-QUIET window are reported (open items).
6. **The calibration's classes and differences** are read from O's 6-decimal label files (AD5
   names unpatched runs, which write nothing else); the floor 1e-6 is above the rounding.
7. **The hub's in-place call** is not reproducible: its outputs (5 runs) are a distribution under
   `label_propagation_hub/inplace/`, outside the manifest, like the in_place set; the inputs and
   both damped calls are hashed.
8. **The GCC 13 compile of the recorder** uses the GCC 13.4 compiler of the conda package cache
   with its libstdc++ headers over the system glibc headers (compile only; no linkable GCC 13 is
   installed, R030.md:83).
9. **Commit `1baf5f1`** carries two things, the certify change its message names and the
   records of the step's last commit-order item (ADR 0063, GOVERNANCE.md, this retrospective,
   the CHANGELOG, plan.md), which were already staged; history is not rewritten, so this line
   records it.

### Deviations from the plan and the step's text (each with the same intent)

1. **The step ran with step 1's STOP open**, as the orchestrator started it; the STOP's row in
   GOVERNANCE.md's `M10 (AD)` sub-block records it and stays pending the author.
2. **The recorder gained `check_labels`** (`--wrap=snprintf` in checker.o, passed on to
   `vsnprintf`) and a tag filter: O prints its mean error with three digits, which cannot show
   agreement to 1e-9. Without `--check` nothing changes (check_twins passes; the goldens were
   exported without `--check`).
3. **The isolation check** (`check_model_isolation.cmake`) gained an optional `ALLOWED` list of
   further dyng/testing headers (lp_host_model uses union_find); the earlier checks are unchanged.
4. **union_find is header-only** (no object file of its own; its symbols are inline).
5. **The generator refuses fractions outside [0, 1]** (O does not check them; no corpus case
   reaches them; ADR 0067).
6. **The 5m harmonic_solution is slower than O's checker** (220 s against 123 s summed over the
   batches at 8 threads: it starts its threads per reduction); it is a test oracle, run at the
   release and on the 50k weekly job, so this was left as it is.
7. **The model's V_aff follows the definition** (N_t(Del_eff) ∪ Ins_eff ∪ N_{t+1}(Ins_eff)); O's
   device also marks the endpoints of a new edge between two vertices present before the batch
   and the neighbours of an id deleted twice. No corpus case reaches either (ADR 0068, "Known
   limits"); the kit's batch axis "edges between present vertices" (step 5) must take it into
   account.
