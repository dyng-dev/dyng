### R031's open item 6: the compute-sanitizer process death (criterion 2; ADR 0112)

Investigated as R031 asked (ADR 0112, "Investigation" and "Results"): the three racecheck commands
that died before (cycle_count's CUDA suite with `DYNG_TEST_SEEDS=2`, the CBST scenario tests with
`DYNG_CBST_REDUCED=1`, the triad kit's C1 / C11 / C12) ran 33 times on GPU 1 from the parity-cuda
build, each through a wrapper that records the test process's own exit status, with a monitor
of GPU 1 and the host: 18 runs on an otherwise idle GPU, 15 with a second CUDA process beside the
sanitizer (part of them under a full CPU build, load average up to 66). **All 33 ended normally**
(exit 0, 0 hazards, no failed test); the outcome has now not reproduced in 52 re-runs. The logs of
R030 and R031 that "printed nothing" do not date the death (block-buffered output is lost when a
process ends by a signal; these runs print nothing either until they exit). The cause stays
unknown and outside the repository's code (the sanitizer's process tree or an outside event), so
it is **fixed under ADR 0112** by a bounded retry of exactly that outcome: every compute-sanitizer
call of `ci/gpu_local.sh` goes through `sanitize`; a racecheck run whose output has "process
didn't terminate successfully", 0 hazards in every summary, no other error or warning and no
failed test runs once more, once; anything else, and a second death, fails; the summary lists the
retries below its table (the step's run above: none). The records and the loop driver are in
`$DYNG_SCRATCH/runs/r040-stability/racecheck/`.
