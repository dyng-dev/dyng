# M3 parity and performance record: the framework migration

Date: 2026-09-29. M3 moves `sssp` and `cycle_count` onto the framework layer
(`cpp/src/framework`, PLAN Sections 4.5.1-4.5.5) one commit at a time (PLAN Section 6.3 step 8).
After every commit the golden parity is re-run, and a commit that touches a gated hot path gets a
performance spot check. This file is the log of those checks. The final gate suites of the
milestone (acceptance criterion 3) are recorded in their own section when they are run.

## 1. `sssp` onto the enactors (step migrate-sssp)

The pre-migration code is `f664f96` (the framework extraction on top of `019ef13`; the library
behaves as `019ef13` apart from the empty budget-counter calls). Its `dyng-compat-mosp` was built
from a `git archive` copy under `$DYNG_SCRATCH/m3-sssp/base` (`parity` and `parity-cuda` presets)
and is the "before" side of every spot check.

### 1.1 Protocol of the checks

- **Parity (every commit).** `parity/compare.py` replays the 495 golden cases of
  MOSP-OpenMP@c352151 byte for byte: `sequential`, `openmp:4` and `sequential/int64` on the
  `parity` preset; `cuda` on the `parity-cuda` preset, GPU 1. Plus `ctest -L cpu` on `dev` (and
  `ctest -L gpu` on `dev-cuda` for the commits that touch the CUDA path).
- **Performance (commits that touch a gated hot path).** `parity/perf_ab.py run` against the
  unpatched original on roadNet-CA, all three batches (safe50k, unsafe50k, local10k), under the
  exclusive perf lock: OpenMP with 28 threads pinned to cores (11 rounds), CUDA on GPU 0 at locked
  clocks (`--lock-clocks boost`, ADR 0018; 21 rounds, the regions are under 10 ms). The dynG
  medians are compared with the same run of the "before" build (section 1.2).
