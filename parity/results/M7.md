## 2. Step sssp-operators: the operators engine against the fused engine (informational)

`parity/perf_ab.py engines --exe build/parity-cuda/tools/compat/dyng-compat-mosp --gpu 0 --runs 21
--graph <g>` under the exclusive perf lock: the same `dyng-compat-mosp` alternates
`--cuda-engine fused` (A) and `--cuda-engine operators` (B), A/B/A/B, 21 accepted rounds per
batch, GPU clocks locked to boost (SM 1695 MHz, memory 7601 MHz) for the whole A/B with every busy
GPU sample at the locked clocks (ADR 0018's protocol), the contamination monitor of `run`; the
outputs of both engines were byte-identical for every batch and the `invalidated` counters equal
in every round. The region is one objective's update: `sssp.enact_fused` (A) and
`sssp.identify_affected + seed + loop + finalize` (B), host times (the operators engine's device
time is within 0.05 ms of its host time). Batches of the original bench (seed 777). Records:
`M7-engines-cuda-<graph>.json`. **No gate** (M7 acceptance criterion 1; PLAN 4.5.4: the
operators engine is the fallback of a device without cooperative launch).

Run-to-run spreads 0-4 % on every per-objective region. The end-to-end runs differ by 0.1-3.7 %
(the engine is a small part of a run that reads text files and applies the batch on the host).

Reading. The operators engine runs the fused engine's algorithm phase by phase (the round counts
above are the operators engine's; the fused one's are of the same size), so the difference is the cost of
leaving the GPU between rounds: one host round trip per near-far round, about 8-9 us each on
this machine (roadNet-PA local10k: 5.8 ms more over 644 rounds; road_usa local10k: 9.4 ms over
1186 rounds), plus the launch latency of the per-round kernels. Where an update has many short
rounds (the local batches, the small road graphs) the engine is 1.4-1.6x the fused one; where
rounds carry much work (rgg, the road_usa 50K batches) 1.03-1.09x. A later step could keep the
near-far loop on the device for several rounds between synchronizations (or capture the rounds
in a CUDA graph) if the fallback's speed ever matters; the plan's 0.2 estimate of "within 1.05x
of the fused one" (docs/developer/plan.md) is met only on rgg and is not an approved gate.
