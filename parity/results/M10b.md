## 1. Step operators-engine: AD6's c, the operators engine, C4 and the engine reading

### 1.1 The calibration of c (frozen before the operators engine; ADR 0101)

AD6 (decided by the author on 2026-10-28): a refactored engine meets max |dF| <= c·ε per batch
against the fused engine's damped bits, ε = 2^-23, with identical per-batch iterations,
components and frontier_visits; c = 3 x the largest max |dF| / ε that perturbing only the
association and the contraction of dynG's fused damped engine produces over every batch of P,
rounded up to a power of two, frozen before the refactor.

- **Tool:** `compare.py label_propagation calibrate` (`parity/label_propagation_engines.py`,
  commit `7f5e156`, its flag fix `cc9641b`). It exports `git archive HEAD` and builds
  `dyng-compat-dynlp` once per variant in one tree (the parity-cuda preset; the compatibility
  tools' algorithms only): a variant sets the new cache variable `DYNG_LP_CALIBRATION_CUDA_FLAGS`,
  which only label_propagation's CUDA sources take (empty in every preset and release build).
  The variants: `control` (no extra flag), `no_fma` (`-fmad=false`: every a·b + c of the engine a
  separate multiply and add; an explicit `-fmad=false` wins over `--use_fast_math`'s
  `-fmad=true`, checked on the compile line), `permuted` (`DYNG_CALIBRATION_LP_PERMUTED_REDUCTION`
  in `propagation.cu`: the lane trees with offsets 1, 2, 4, ... instead of L/2, ..., 1, and the
  block's warp sums added from the last warp) and `both`. Every batch of P (the nine non-digest
  cases of P+: 5k5, 50k, 500k at δ 1e-4; 5k5, 50k at δ 1e-6; 5k5, 50k at seeds 1 and 2), damped,
  `--cuda-engine fused --label-dump`, GPU 1, against the twin's per-batch label bits.
- **Record:** `parity/results/M10b-calibration.json` (port commit `cc9641b`; SHA-256
  `10bc2fd7...aa8e`, frozen in `parity/goldens.toml` `[sets.label_propagation_damped.refactored]`
  with c; `frozen_bound()` refuses a c that is not the record's, or a changed record).
- **The control is exact:** every batch's bits and every count of the nine cases equal the
  golden's (the reduced build is the engine).
- **Result:** max |dF| = **2.411e-2** (`permuted`, 500k, batch 1) = 202,210.75 ε; 3 x that =
  606,632 ε, so **c = 2^20 = 1,048,576** and **c·ε = 0.125**.
- **Every perturbation changed a count** (27 of the 27 perturbed runs: frontier_visits in every
  case, iterations in some): a refactored engine whose arithmetic is not the fused engine's to the
  bit cannot keep AD6's identical counts on P (it predicts AD6's non-default fallback for such an
  engine). The perturbed labels move by up to 2.4e-2 because a vertex whose change crosses δ in
  one run and not in the other changes the frontier, and the stopping rule then leaves the labels
  at different points.
- The first run of the calibration (on `7f5e156`) built `both` without its `-fmad=false` (a list
  inside an unquoted generator expression split it; fixed in `cc9641b`, checked on the compile
  line); it was run again in full on `cc9641b`; only the second run is the record.

### 1.2 The operators (ADR 0102)

`binned_frontier`, `neighbor_reduce` and `connected_components` in
`cpp/src/algorithms/label_propagation/operators/` (the rule of two). Their tests
(`dyng_label_propagation_operators_tests`, label cpu; `dyng_label_propagation_operators_cuda_tests`,
label gpu): on sequential_exec against independent models, and on cuda_exec against the sequential
model: neighbor_reduce's averages **bit for bit on every mapping** (8 lanes, a warp, blocks of 256
and 1024) over 6,008 vertices with the hubs of degrees 8, 9, 32, 33, 1024, 1025, 3000 and 6000;
the device pushes (concurrent, every vertex three times) and expand on 8, 32, 256 and 1024 lanes
give the host's sets per bin and half; Shiloach-Vishkin gives the host's canonical ids (up to
200,000 ids, 110,000 edges) with one host synchronization per round and one for the count.
All passed (dev-cuda, GPU 1; and in the fortified and `DYNG_ENABLE_OPENMP=OFF` CPU builds).

### 1.3 The operators engine against the fused engine (AD6; criterion 2)

- **`compare.py label_propagation operators` on P+** (`parity/results/M10b-operators.json`, the
  parity-cuda build of `100dbb2`, GPU 1): every one of the 10 cases (5m by its per-batch
  digests) **within c·ε with max |dF| = 0 on every batch** (the operators engine's label bits are
  the fused engine's, so the golden's) and **identical per-batch iterations, components and
  frontier_visits**; the `dynlp_labels.txt` bytes equal too. AD6's pre-agreed fallback is not
  needed: no ADR of a first diverging batch, the bound not loosened, `engine::automatic` stays
  the fused engine.
- **The hub golden on the operators engine** (`LabelPropagationCuda.OperatorsEngineHubGolden`):
  the original's damped bits (SHA-256 `c1089fca...`), 2,055 iterations, 14,907,520
  frontier visits and next tag 2,057, twice (its persistent iterations are 0: no persistent
  kernel).
- **The published flow under strict budgets** (`OperatorsEngineIsTheFusedEnginesBitsAndCounts`:
  5k5 at delta 1e-4 and 1e-6, 20k x 6): every batch's label bits and every counter (iterations,
  frontier_visits, components, sparsified edges, initial frontier, affected, converged,
  fallback_used, the apply summary) equal the fused engine's; the declared host synchronizations
  hold. `OperatorsEngineLimitsAndScope`: the cap under `report`, `fallback_recompute` (IrLP) and
  `error` as the fused engine; `in_place` refused before anything changes; `engine::automatic`
  stays fused; after `reserve()` no device allocation from the third batch of 50k on.
- **The checker oracles on the operators engine** (`LabelPropagationChecker.DampedOperators5k5`
  and `DampedOperators50k`, cuda): union_find, V_aff, lp_host_model, the harmonic oracle at
  delta 1e-6 (and IrLP's snapshots at 5k5): passed.
- **The kit's C4** (`dyng_label_propagation_conformance_cuda_tests`, both graph types): the damped
  suite compares the two engines after compute() and three batches for every preset, the small
  and medium sizes and every vertex-batch mix: **passed (bitwise, the declared level of the damped
  suite)**; the in_place suite skips C4 (the operators engine has no in_place schedule).

### 1.4 The mutations on the operators engine (AD9)

The CTests `label_propagation.mutation.cuda_operators.<name>` run each mutant on the operators
engine's own cases alone (the checker at 5k5 on it and the `OperatorsEngine*` cases), dev-cuda,
GPU 1: the control passes; `damped_no_damping`, `lp_d_a_unfiltered_components`,
`lp_d_b_affected_too_small`, `lp_d_c_stale_buffer` and `lp_d_e_resurrected` **all fail** (each
detected). `inplace_plain_jacobi` acts on `schedule::in_place`, which the operators engine does not
run: it has no row there (its host and fused rows are unchanged and detected). All 20 CTests of
`ctest -R label_propagation.mutation` passed (each mutant detected, each control passing).

### 1.6 The STOP: the in_place cloud missed by two 50k runs

`compare.py label_propagation inplace --cases 5k5,50k` (`M10b-inplace-cuda.json`): at 50k run 0
is outside at batches 6 and 8 (265 and 109 iterations against the envelope's 258.5 and 104.5) and
run 1 at batch 9 (84 against 78.1); run 2 inside. Every run's labels are within T_mean, T_max and
T_flips of every reference run, its components and initial frontier exact, its iteration sum
inside. M10b changed no code the fused in_place path runs, and its kernels are the original's
instruction for instruction. The 30-run evidence at 50k (`M10b-inplace-rate-50k.json`, fresh runs
on GPU 1): the original outside in 0 of 30, the port in 2 of 30 (batch 9: 80; batch 6: 265); with
M10a's 30-run evidence the original is outside in 3 of 60 and the port in 3 of 60 (the original's
own outside runs have the same batch-6 count, 265). Nothing was relaxed, re-rolled or replaced;
the reading goes to the author (GOVERNANCE.md, Open decisions, `M10 (AD)`).

### 1.7 The engine reading (reported, never gated)

are equal (**0 ulps**) and every round's counts and label bytes passed the guard.
